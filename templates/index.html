About UrbanPulse Hub

UrbanPulse Hub is a community-driven Smart City Development Survey platform designed to collect real-time data about urban infrastructure conditions at the grassroots level.

What We Do

  • Collect structured data on water supply, road conditions, street lighting, waste management, and drainage issues
  • Empower residents to voice their concerns through a simple, accessible digital form
  • Aggregate survey data to help local authorities prioritize development projects
  • Enable data-driven decision making for smarter urban planning

How It Works

  • Residents fill out a quick survey about their street's infrastructure
  • Data is stored securely (locally or via Supabase cloud database)
  • Survey results can be viewed and analyzed for actionable insights
  • Authorities can identify problem areas and allocate resources efficiently

Technology

Built with modern web technologies including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Three.js 3D rendering, and Supabase for real-time data storage. The platform features an interactive 3D animated cityscape, particle effects, light/dark theme, and a responsive glassmorphism UI.

Project Benefits & Overview

UrbanPulse Hub transforms how communities interact with urban development by bridging the gap between citizens and local governance.

Key Benefits

  • Real-Time Data Collection: Instant gathering of infrastructure status across multiple streets and neighborhoods
  • Transparent Governance: Open data visibility helps build trust between residents and authorities
  • Rapid Issue Identification: Pinpoint exact locations with drainage, lighting, or road problems
  • Resource Optimization: Help authorities prioritize repairs based on actual community needs
  • Community Empowerment: Every resident's voice contributes to neighborhood improvement
  • Digital Accessibility: Works on any device — mobile, tablet, or desktop
  • Zero Paperwork: Completely digital survey process saves time and environment
  • Historical Tracking: Monitor improvement over time with stored survey records

Impact Areas

  • Water Supply Management
  • Road Maintenance & Safety
  • Street Lighting Coverage
  • Waste Collection Efficiency
  • Drainage & Flood Prevention
  • Public Health & Sanitation

Vision

To create a digitally connected community where every citizen plays an active role in shaping their city's future — one survey at a time.
